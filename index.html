About

I'm a penetration tester working on web applications, APIs and internal networks. My engagements are a mix of grey-box and black-box, and I combine manual testing in Burpsuite with automated tools for coverage.

I started in security as a junior penetration tester, then spent two years as a SOC analyst monitoring systems, triaging alerts and running vulnerability and configuration assessments. That time on the defensive side shapes how I write findings: clear impact, clear fix. I also train and mentor people who are entering the field.
